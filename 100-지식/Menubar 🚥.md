###### 1. What do the arrows in menubar do?
The arrows in the menubar act like arrows in browsers and take you back and forth between the pages you open within Scrintal. You can go back to any doc or board you opened or to different sections in the app, such as the learning center and profile pages if you click on them as well. 
![image]()
###### 2. What does the star in the menubar do?
The star on the menubar is for starring boards or docs. One-click on the star will place this item under the starred section on the sidebar. Clicking on the star after the selection is done will remove the star and the item from the sidebar.

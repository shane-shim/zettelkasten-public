**[0:04]** **Creating cards** in Scrintal is a breeze, and there are multiple ways to do it:
- **Button on the Left-Hand Side**: Click the first button on the left side of your desk. Choose the "card" option, and your cursor will transform into a card shape. Place it anywhere on your desk to create a new card instantly.
- **Double-Click**: Double-click on any empty space on your desk, and a new card will appear immediately.
- **Keyboard Shortcut**: Press the "C" key on your keyboard, and your cursor will turn into a card shape. Click anywhere on your desk to create a new card.
- **Right-Click**: Simply right-click on your desk and select "Create new Card" from the menu to generate a new card.
- **URL Shortcut**: Use the URL shortcut: `…/app/card/create?title=YourNewCardTitleHere` to create a new card with your desired title.
**[0:34]** **Text Formatting:**
The text editor in Scrintal supports various formatting options, including headings (H1, H2, H3), plain text, bold, italic, strikethrough, underline, inline code, external and internal links, code blocks, block quotes, ordered and unordered lists.
**[1:18] Card Content and Embedding:**
You have a variety of options to enhance your card content:
- Embed media such as YouTube and Vimeo videos, Twitter tweets, images (JPG, PNG), and PDFs. To include multimedia content, simply paste the links into the text editor, and it will automatically detect and display them correctly.
- You can upload a wide range of file types, including audio files, video files, and text files. Use the insert menu by typing a slash (/) or drag and drop to upload files directly into the text editor.
**[2:00]** **Creating Linked Cards:**
Linked cards offer an effective way to connect related information:
- To create a Linked Card, type a plus (+) sign followed by the title of the card you want to reference. As you start typing, a dropdown menu will appear, displaying cards and boards from your archive. Select the one you want to link to. Alternatively, you can add a plus (+) sign in front of any word and press enter to create a Linked Card. If the card title doesn't exist, a new card with that title will be created.
- For a more visual approach, drag the purple dot from Card A to Card B on your desk or board to establish a direct link between them. To create a new card (Card C) and link it automatically, drag the purple dot to an empty space on your board. Linked cards are represented with arrows, showing the direction of the link.
Now that you're armed with these card creation and formatting skills, you're well on your way to maximizing your productivity with Scrintal!

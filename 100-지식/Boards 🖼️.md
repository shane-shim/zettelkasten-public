###### 1. What is a board?
Boards are arrangements of your docs. They are good for categorizing docs, freezing, and retrieving a visualization. They are also great for preparing presentations or to share your notes with others.
![image]()
###### 2. How do I create a board?
There are five ways to create a board;
1. You can create a board by pressing the Create a board (third button) on the left side of your desk. This will create a new empty board.
2. Another way to create a board is to select the docs you would like to have on your board and then select create board option in the action bar displayed.
3. You can right-click on top of one or multiple docs, and select the “create a board” option.
4. You can go to the “Docs” browse view in your left sidebar, activate the function “select docs,” choose the docs you want to place in your new board, click the “options” button, and click “Create a board with docs.”
- You can create a new board by using the URL shortcut: `…/app/board/create?title=A new board title here`
###### 3. How can I find a board?
You can find a list of all your boards on the sidebar under the “Boards“ browse view. You can click on a board to go to that board. Alternatively, you can search for a board’s title using the spotlight feature [CMD+Shift+K] / [CTRL+Shift+K]. Here, boards and docs are displayed with different icons.
###### 4. How do I navigate between boards?
When managing multiple boards, you can efficiently switch between them by utilising the back-and-forth arrows conveniently located on the menu bar. For enhanced organization, especially when dealing with interconnected boards, consider linking them together. To do this, while in Board A, locate Board B in the left sidebar, and simply drag and drop the link onto Board A. This way, you establish a quick reference point for easy navigation between the two boards, repeating the process in board B if needed.
###### 5. How do I Publish a Board?
You can publish a board using the publish and share button next to the board’s title. You will be generating a secret URL that you can send to those you would like to share a “read-only” view of your board.
![image]()
###### 6. How do I update a Public Board?
You can make any changes you want in your boards at any time, even after your boards are published. To update the public board, hit the “Publish board” button again and the published version will be updated. The URL link will remain the same.
###### 7. How do I share a board?
You can share a board and work collaboratively with another Scrintal user. You can share a board using the publish and share button next to the board’s title. Insert the emails of those you would like to co-work with (synchronically or asynchronously) in your board and then press share.
###### 8. How can I delete a board?
You can delete a board by clicking the three dots next to the Spotlight button. This action won’t delete the docs placed inside the board, it will only delete this particular board.
If you’d like to delete the docs in a board as well, first select all docs (CMD/CTRL+A) and then right-click, and select delete. After this action, you can also delete the board.

###### 1. What are columns?
A column is an element to organize and categorize your docs visually. A column only has a visual function; once they are removed from the board/desk, they disappear. The columns are not stored in the library.
![image]()
###### 2. How can I create columns?
You can create columns in the following two ways:
- You can create a column by pressing the first button on the left-hand side of the desk. You can select the column option, and your cursor will change its shape. You can then place your cursor anywhere you want to see your new column.
- Press K on your keyboard to activate the creation. Your cursor will change its shape. You can then place your cursor anywhere you want to see your new column.
Keep on adding as many columns as you need by clicking once on a column and then clicking on the “add column” option.
###### 3. Are docs in columns linked?
Docs placed in a column keep their links, but the visual linking is removed. If you place linked docs in columns, you will see no links, yet the links among the docs will still be there. When you remove these docs from the columns, you will see the links visualized again.
###### 4. How can I remove just one column from my columns?
You can remove one column from your columns by dragging it out of the columns’ frame. After you do this, you can click once on the column you took out of the frame and then press the backspace or right-click> library.
###### 5. How can I merge two columns?
You can simply merge them by dragging one column on the other. You can organize the columns as you please by moving the columns to the right or left to create the order you are looking for.
###### 6. How can I remove a column from my board/desk?
There are two ways to remove a column from your board/desk:
- Click once on the column and press backspace on your keyboard
- Right-click on the top of the columns and select “remove from board/desk”
###### 7. What’s the text function?
Free text is a visual element whose function is entirely visual. Once they are removed from the board/desk, they disappear. The free text removed from the board/desk is not stored in the library.
![image]()
###### 8. How do I use the text function?
You can create the text element in the following two ways:
- You can create the text by pressing the first button on the left-hand side of the desk. You can select the text option, and your cursor will change its shape. You can then place your cursor anywhere you want to see your new text.
- Press T on your keyboard to activate the creation. Your cursor will change its shape. You can then place your cursor anywhere you want to see your new text.
###### 9. Is the text stored in my library?
No, text is non-searchable, and it is not stored in your library. Text is a visual element to use on display on your desk and boards.
###### 10. Can I link the text to my regular docs?
No, you can’t. The text is purely visual. The text element cannot be linked to your docs.

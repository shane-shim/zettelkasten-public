# 튜닝 prompt

#100-지식

Situation: Collaborating with experts in marketing, psychology, linguistics, and cultural studies to create innovative copywriting that resonates with a diverse clientele. The goal is to reflect varied customer needs and lifestyles while emphasizing the product's unique selling propositions. 
Task: Generate at least five compelling copywrites. These should succinctly convey the product's key features and benefits, engage the customer's interest, and evoke empathy. The copywrites must be emotionally and persuasively charged, making the product feel indispensable for solving everyday problems.
Intent: The core intent is to craft messages that underscore the product's role in addressing specific day-to-day challenges, thereby positioning it as an essential item in the consumer's life. The messaging should connect emotionally, highlighting the product's multifunctionality and ease of use, within a strict limit of 10 words per copywrite. 
Concern: It's critical to maintain message clarity and brevity, reflecting actual issues and emotions faced by consumers in their daily lives. The challenge lies in balancing multifunctionality and simplicity, ensuring the copywrites form a strong empathetic bond with the target audience without exceeding the word limit. Calibration: Final copywrites should vividly mirror real-life scenarios, focusing on the specific solutions the product offers. Through a careful selection of words and phrases, these messages aim to fortify the consumer's emotional and practical connection to the product, making it a perceived necessity.

Process: Engage in Creative Brainstorming: Leverage the team's interdisciplinary expertise to pinpoint everyday problems and emotional touchpoints relevant to our target audience. 

Craft and Refine Messages: Using insights from our brainstorming session, construct concise, powerful copywrites that encapsulate the product's benefits and emotional appeal within a 10-word limit. 

Evaluate and Iterate: Critically assess each copywrite for its emotional resonance, clarity, and alignment with consumer lifestyles. Iterate based on feedback to ensure each message is impactful and meets our stringent criteria. This structured approach ensures the development of targeted, effective copywriting that speaks directly to the consumer's needs, aspirations, and challenges, ultimately fostering a deeper connection between the customer and the product.

명령어 예시 
"진한 화장 없이 자연광채 가지고 싶은 30대 이상의 여성"을 위한 "호호바 프로텍션 선스크린" 제품의 How를 5개 이상 작성해줘. "호호바 프로텍션 선스크린"은 자외선만 보호해주는게 아니라 영양 수분크림 80%가 들어가 있어 피부회복에도 효과가 좋아. 이 특징은 매우 중요한 것이니 꼭 참고해줘.

###### 1. What is a doc?
A doc is a container of text (i.e. a note)
###### 2. What does a doc include?
- A doc on a desk has its title and its content, which is clipped to fit a specific doc size.
- When a doc’s title or content changes, it will be automatically updated on the desk.
![image]()
###### 3. How do I create a doc?
There are five ways to create docs;
- You can create a doc by pressing the first button on the left-hand side of the desk. You can select the doc option, and your cursor will take the shape of a doc. You can then place your cursor anywhere you want to see your new doc.
- Double-click any empty place on your desk to create a new doc immediately.�
- Press C on your keyboard to activate the creation. Your cursor will take the shape of a doc. You can then place your cursor anywhere you want to see your new doc.
- Right-click on the desk and select the “Create new Doc” option.
- You can create a new doc by using the URL shortcut: `…/app/doc/create?title=A new doc title here`
![image]()
###### 4. How do I create a Daily Doc?
There are two ways to create daily docs;
- You can create a daily doc by pressing the first button on the left-hand side of the desk. You can select the daily doc option, and your cursor will take the shape of a doc. You can then place your cursor anywhere you want to see your new doc.
- You can press D on your keyboard to activate the creation. Your cursor will take the shape of a doc. You can then place your cursor anywhere you want to see your daily doc.
Daily docs come with today’s date as a title.
###### 5. How can I write on a doc?
You can immediately start writing a doc’s title by clicking once on it. Doc titles have a maximum limit of 140 characters. For more writing-related tips, please check the text editor functions.
###### 6. How can I delete a doc?
- You can delete a doc in three ways:
    - You can click once on a doc and activate the action bar. Then, you can click on the three dots on the action bar and select the delete option.�
    - Right-click on the doc while on the desk or board and select the option Delete.
    - In the sidebar's "Docs" section, find your doc, click “options,” and select "delete" to delete your doc.
- Deleted docs are moved to the Recently Deleted section and stay there for an additional 30 days before they are permanently deleted.
![image]()
###### 7. How can I edit a doc?
- You can double-click on a doc to edit the doc’s content. The doc will open, and you can start editing. When a doc is open, we call this a “Doc Window.”
- You can open and edit multiple docs at the same time by selecting the area of the docs and using the shortcut: Option + O (MacOS) and Alt + O (Others).
###### 8. What do the Locate, Maximise, and Close buttons do?
- The buttons on the top right corner of each open doc are called Locate, Maximise, and Close buttons.
    - If you click on a doc’s Maximise button, you will go to focus writing mode.
    - If you click on a doc’s Locate button, the desk or board will center on the doc. The locate button also adds the opened docs to the desk or board if the doc is not on the desk.
    - If you click on the Close button, the doc window will be closed.
![image]()
###### 9. How do I add an already open doc to my desk/board (e.g., for docs opened with the Spotlight)?
If an open doc is not already on your desk, you can click the Locate button on the top right of the doc to add it to your desk. The desk will scroll to the newly added doc automatically.
###### 10. How can I export my doc?
You can download your doc as .md or save it as PDF. You can export your docs in three ways;
- Action bar: You can click once on a doc and activate the action bar. Then, you can select download as .md or save as PDF options.
- Double-click: You can double-click and open the doc and press the 3 dots icon on the upper right-hand side. Then, you can select download as .md or save as PDF options.
- Sidebar: In the sidebar's "Docs" section, find your doc, click “options,” and select "Download .md" to export in Markdown format.

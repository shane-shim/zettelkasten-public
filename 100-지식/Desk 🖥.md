The desk is where you will spend most of your time. It’s designed as a virtual desk where you can bring docs via search and work with them.
![image]()
###### 1. How can I remove docs from the desk?
###### If you’d like to remove docs from the desk, you can remove them. There are three ways to remove docs;
- by selecting the docs or selecting the area of the docs you’d like to remove and then pressing the backspace or delete keys on the keyboard.
- by selecting the docs on the desk, right-click and select the “Remove from desk” option.
- by clicking once on a doc and activating the action bar. Then click on the three dots and select the remove option.
Removing docs from the desk does not mean the docs are deleted. The docs can be found in the remove by using [Search 🔍](Search%20%F0%9F%94%8D.md) If you want to delete the docs permanently, you can either click the “three-dots” icon in the top right corner of a doc and select the delete option or select the docs on the desk, right-click, and select the delete option.
###### 2. How can I navigate the Desk?
You can navigate by using the “Toggle Pan” on the left-hand side of the desk. The Toggle Pan is activated by clicking on it. If it’s activated, clicking on it again will deactivate it.
- When this function is active, you can drag your mouse on the desk to navigate the desk. This function is especially useful if you use an external mouse instead of a touchpad.
- When the drag tool is activated, you cannot click any doc or use the selection rectangle.
- You can also activate drag by pressing and holding the spacebar while you are dragging the desk. Once you release the spacebar, the drag tool will deactivate automatically.
- You can also activate drag by pressing the M key on your keyboard.
###### 3. What does the “Organize Docs” function do?
The function “Organize Docs” on the context menu that pops up when you right-click on your desk organizes the docs automatically with Scrintal’s built-in algorithm, placing the docs with the highest number of links at the center.
###### 4. What does the “Fit All to Screen” do?
The function “Fit All to Screen” on the context menu that pops up when you right-click on your desk fits all the docs on display to one screen size view. This function is especially recommended when you have many docs on a board or desk and also when you’d like to publish boards.

###### 5. How do I see the links of a doc on my desk?
When you click once on a doc on your desk, the action bar appears. The furthest left option in the action bar is the **show links option**. When you click on show links, the search will appear on the sidebar. You can then drag & drop any doc further links to your desk or board.
You can also click once on a doc on your desk and open the right sidebar next to the spotlight function.
Alternatively, you can open your doc, scroll all the way, and find the list of links and backlinks.
If you want to see all the links on your desk, open the right sidebar next to the Spotlight button and all the docs and links will be listed.
###### 6. How can I clear my desk or board?
If you’d like to remove all your docs from the desk or board, you can right-click on the desk or board and select the option “clear desk/board.”
###### 7. What’s the difference between remove and delete?
When you remove a doc, you remove the doc from the board/desk you are working on. You can perform this action by selecting the doc(s) and pressing backspace on your keyboard.
When you delete a doc, you are deleting it from your entire system. You won’t see this doc in any of your boards or in your library anymore. Scrintal has a trash bin at the bottom of the sidebar.

# What can I use Public boards for

#100-지식 #scrintal_help

#scrintal_help
You can use Public Boards to share information within your teams, close collaborators, and clients, or publicly with a broader audience in your social media for example.
Here are some examples to spark some inspiration:
- Create your Professional Portfolio and share it on social media. [<u>Check out this example</u>](https://beta.scrintal.com/b/designer-portfolio-template--xww2a).
- Introduce a Project, [<u>check out how we introduce Scrintal</u>](https://beta.scrintal.com/b/final-scrintal-board--jtult).
- Share company information with your colleagues, [<u>check out how we shared a template on company information</u>](https://beta.scrintal.com/b/pitch--samq4).
- For Kanban boards and Project Planning, check out our [<u>Planning Board</u>](https://beta.scrintal.com/b/planning-board--hu9yl).

###### 1. How can I export my notes?
You can download your doc as .md or save it as PDF. You can export your docs in three ways;
- Action bar: You can click once on a doc and activate the action bar. Then, you can select download as .md or save as PDF options.
- Double-click: You can double-click and open the doc and press the 3 dots icon on the upper right-hand side. Then, you can select download as .md or save as PDF options.
- Sidebar: In the sidebar's "Docs" section, find your doc, click “options”, and select "Download .md" to export in Markdown format.

###### 2. Can I export my entire library?
Absolutely, you can easily export your entire library with just a few steps. First, head to your left sidebar and select "docs" browse view. From there, locate the "Export All" option. When you click on it, the system will diligently compile all your docs and boards into markdown format, bundling them into a zip file for your convenience. Your boards will be transformed into markdown files, preserving all the essential references as clickable links. Be patient as this operation may require some time, depending on the size of your library.
![image]()
###### 2. How can I import my MD archive?
Click on My Docs under the Browse section of the sidebar. Click the 3 dots in the top right corner (next to the Spotlight button).  Select the “Import docs” option. Select a directory that contains  `.md` files you would like to import.
Once uploaded, your files will be saved as a new individual doc, and inside each doc, you will find the tag `#imported` to locate them in your library easily.
> <u>Important Note:</u> You must import a Markdown archive, a directory that contains one or more Markdown (`.md`) files. You cannot select individual Markdown files. Embedded elements (files, images) will not be imported; your tags and bidirectional links will be imported.

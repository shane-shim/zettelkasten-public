This comprehensive guide will take you through the essential aspects of utilizing boards in Scrintal, covering creating, organizing, and sharing boards effectively.
**[0:03] Create a Board**
- Create a board using various methods, including the "Create a board" button on the left side of your desk, selecting cards and creating a board from them, right-clicking on cards to create a board, or using URL shortcuts.
- Easily access your boards in the sidebar's "Boards" browse view or use the spotlight search feature for quick navigation.
- Efficiently switch between boards with back-and-forth arrows on the menu bar and link interconnected boards for easy reference.
**[0:53] Create Columns**
- Create columns by clicking the first button on the left-hand side of the desk, selecting the column option, and placing it where you want.
- Alternatively, press "K" on your keyboard to activate column creation and place it where desired.
- Manage columns by adding, removing, or merging them to organize your workspace efficiently.
**[1:30] Add Text on Board**
- Free text elements are visual and serve a purely visual function. When removed from the board/desk, they disappear and are not stored in the archive.
**[2:50] Add Links to Boards**
- You can link boards to other boards by pressing the "(+)" button on the left side of your board, creating a new board. To connect existing boards, search for the desired board, and drag and drop it onto the target board.
**[3:42] Share/Publish Boards**
- Publish a board using the "publish and share" button next to the board's title, generating a secret URL for a "read-only" view.
- You can make updates to a published board at any time by clicking the "Publish board" button again, with the URL link remaining the same.
- Collaborate with other Scrintal users by sharing a board using the "publish and share" button, inserting their emails for synchronous or asynchronous collaboration.
This guide equips you with the knowledge and tools to effectively utilize Scrintal's board features, enhancing your organization and collaboration capabilities within the platform.

###### 1. How do I open/close the sidebars?
You can easily toggle the sidebars in Scrintal by clicking on the corresponding toggle icons located on the menubar. For the left sidebar, simply click the toggle icon next to the Scrintal logo on the left side of the screen. Likewise, for the right sidebar, you'll find the toggle icon at the far right of your screen.

The Right Sidebar in Scrintal offers a wealth of contextual information and tools tailored to your current view, whether it's a Card, Board, or Web Link.�
Here's a breakdown of what you can discover within the right sidebar:
- **Links:** Explore all Links, Backlinks, Appears On, and Tags associated with your current item. Easily expand nested links and drag-and-drop items directly onto a Board for efficient organization.
